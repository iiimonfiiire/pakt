# Plainspoken style guide

*A minimal example guide. It exists to show that PAKT works with any style guide, not only Signal.*

Plainspoken suits a consumer product with a warm, conversational voice. It is deliberately short and differs from Signal on purpose.

## Voice

- **Contractions** – Use them. They keep the voice relaxed.
- **Sentence length** – Keep sentences under 25 words.
- **Voice** – Prefer active voice. Two passive sentences per document are fine.

## Words

- **Plain words** – Never write "utilize," "leverage," or "in order to." Write "use," "use," and "to."
- **Numbers** – Use numerals for every number, even small ones.

## Formatting

- **Headings** – Use sentence case.
- **Links** – Name the destination. Never write "click here."

## Release notes

Group changes under these headings, in this order: Added, Changed, Fixed, and Removed.
